AN ORIGINAL GAME BY JISHNU GANGULY COMING SOON

All That
Was Left.

Some places hold on to their stories. Others leave behind only fragments. Step into an investigation where the smallest details can matter, the past is never quite out of reach, and the truth has to be pieced together one discovery at a time.

THE EXPERIENCE

Look closer.
Nothing is there by accident.

All That Was Left is a first-person, story-driven investigation game centred around exploration, evidence and the slow process of uncovering what happened. At the heart of the experience is a burned house — a place filled with traces of a story that has not yet been fully understood.

As the investigation unfolds, players explore the environment, examine objects, document discoveries and bring pieces of evidence together. Notes, photographs, records and the details hidden in ordinary spaces all become part of a larger picture.

The experience is designed around curiosity and observation. Instead of simply handing players answers, the game invites them to pay attention, question what they find and work out how the evidence connects.

THE PEOPLE AT THE CENTRE

Different ways of seeing.

Arin and Mira are central to the experience. Their perspectives shape how the investigation is approached, what gets documented and how different pieces of evidence come into view.

01 / ARIN

Arin

Arin's side of the investigation is associated with a distinctly analogue approach to documentation — bringing a different visual texture and perspective to the evidence.

Mira

Mira's perspective brings another dimension to the investigation, with a more contemporary approach to capturing and examining the details that might otherwise be overlooked.
