04 / ABOUT

Not one thing.
Many directions.

I'm Jishnu Ganguly, based in New Delhi, India. This website is a home for the things I make, the subjects I explore and the interests that keep me curious.

A personal space for making music, following aviation, exploring technology and building things for the web. Different interests, one common thread — the joy of figuring out how things work.

I don't think everything needs to fit into one category. Sometimes the most interesting ideas happen somewhere between two completely different worlds.
