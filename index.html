03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Not just one thing.

I like making things, understanding how things work, and finding interesting connections between seemingly unrelated worlds.

I'm Jishnu Ganguly, an independent creator based in New Delhi, India. This website is my own little corner of the internet — a place for the things I make, the things I follow, and the projects I want to put into the world.

Music is one part of it. Aviation is another. Technology, experimentation and creative projects find their way in too.

There's no single category that quite fits everything here. That's probably the point.
