CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT COMING SOON
AN UPCOMING INVESTIGATION GAME

All That
Was Left.

What remains when everything else is gone? All That Was Left is a first-person 3D investigation game built around uncovering information, examining evidence and piecing together a story from what people and places leave behind.

Rather than relying on horror, the experience focuses on observation, atmosphere, discovery and the process of making connections. The world is something to investigate — not simply something to walk through.

Two perspectives.
One investigation.

One of the game's central ideas is the use of two distinct evidence cameras. Arin and Mira approach the investigation through different tools, visual languages and kinds of evidence. The cameras aren't just visual effects; they're part of how the player explores the case.

01 / ARIN

The VHS camera

A 1990s-style camcorder aesthetic, with recording indicators, grain and visual imperfections. A tool associated with corporate and financial clues.

02 / MIRA

The DSLR camera

A modern camera interface with focus and grid elements. A different way of documenting details connected to family and personal evidence.

Built around investigation.
